Binance vs OKX in South Africa (2026)

Last updated: 12 September 2026

For most South Africans the deciding factor between Binance and OKX is how rand gets in and out — not a fraction of a percent on trading fees. Binance currently documents more explicit ZAR funding routes, while OKX has a documented ZAR P2P market for South African users.

Fees and routes change, so treat the figures below as a starting point and confirm the current numbers in each app before acting.

Quick verdict

  • ZAR funding: Binance currently documents more direct rand funding options, including linked-bank ZAR deposits, card and Apple Pay. OKX has a documented ZAR P2P market for South African users; other fiat purchase methods can depend on what is available to your account.
  • Standard spot fees: Very close. OKX lists around 0.08% maker / 0.10% taker for Regular-tier standard spot pairs, while Binance commonly starts around 0.10% for standard spot trading before any applicable BNB discount.
  • FSCA CASP licence: As of our latest check in September 2026, neither Binance nor OKX appears on the FSCA’s published list of authorised CASPs.
  • Choose Binance: if straightforward ZAR funding and broad exchange liquidity matter most to you.
  • Choose OKX: if trading tools, Web3 features and the OKX Card are more important to you.

ZAR access: where they really differ

This is the difference most South African users feel first. Binance supports direct rand funding through a linked South African bank account, card and Apple Pay (Google Pay is not currently supported for ZAR), and it has run ZAR deposit fee campaigns at times.

OKX has supported ZAR P2P for South African users, where you buy from local merchants using payment methods offered by available ZAR merchants. Other fiat purchase methods, including card-based purchases, can depend on the payment provider and what is available to your account.

In short: Binance currently documents more explicit ZAR funding routes, while OKX has a documented ZAR P2P route and other fiat-buy options that should be checked inside your account. For a full walkthrough of moving rand into USDT, see How to buy USDT with ZAR.

Fees: closer than the headlines suggest

At the regular tier, spot fees are essentially in line — OKX lists around 0.08% maker / 0.10% taker for Regular-tier standard spot pairs, while Binance is commonly cited at 0.10% before any applicable BNB discount. Both offer free crypto deposits and network-dependent withdrawal fees. The bigger cost driver for South Africans is usually how you move rand: a card surcharge or a conversion spread can outweigh a small trading-fee difference many times over.

Regulatory status: the same caveat

As of our September 2026 check, neither platform appears on the FSCA’s published authorised-CASP list. Binance does, however, use a separate South African regulatory arrangement for certain derivatives products through Binance-group entities acting as juristic representatives of FiveWest OTC Desk (Pty) Ltd. That should not be confused with Binance itself holding an FSCA CASP licence.

OKX has licences in a number of other jurisdictions, including its MiCA authorisation in Europe, but those overseas licences are not South African FSCA CASP licences.

Two further OKX points worth knowing. OKX offers the OKX Card, available in South Africa as part of its Africa rollout, which can spend supported stablecoins and convert them at the point of purchase. And in February 2025, Aux Cayes FinTech Co. Ltd., an OKX affiliate, pleaded guilty in the United States to operating an unlicensed money-transmitting business and agreed to penalties and forfeiture totalling more than US$500 million. OKX said the case related to legacy compliance gaps involving US users and outlined remedial compliance measures.

If local licensing and a domestic escalation channel matter to you, compare a licensed exchange such as VALR or Luno. See Is Binance legal in South Africa? for the detail.

Which should you choose?

Choose Binance if straightforward ZAR funding and broad exchange liquidity are your main considerations. Choose OKX if trading tools, Web3 features and the OKX Card matter more. If an FSCA CASP licence is your deciding factor, compare a licensed local exchange such as VALR or Luno.
