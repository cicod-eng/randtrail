Risk Disclosure

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Cryptocurrency is highly volatile and involves significant market, custody, counterparty and operational risk. You may lose some or all of the money you invest.

Before you buy crypto

  • Prices can move sharply in either direction.
  • Transactions can be difficult or impossible to reverse.
  • Exchanges can fail, and assets held on an exchange carry custody risk.
  • Scams are common, including on messaging apps.
  • Tax may apply to your gains, and record-keeping is your responsibility.

Do not deposit money simply because a platform is popular, and do not send crypto until you have checked the address and network carefully. RandTrail provides educational information only.
