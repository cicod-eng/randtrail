If the code does not pre-fill, enter GPYBA6R3 manually in the referral-code field before completing registration.

Important: A referral code is normally linked when the account is created. Do not assume you will be able to add or replace one later.

Because fee schedules and referral terms can change, treat the percentage shown on your registration page and in your account as the final word. If Binance displays a different figure for a particular product, that displayed figure governs.

ZAR deposits in South Africa

Binance continues to support South African rand funding and has expanded its local payment options. The exact methods shown to you can vary, so always use the options displayed under Add Funds → Deposit ZAR in your own account.

Bank transfer

Binance published an updated ZAR bank-transfer guide in September 2026. The basic flow is to open Add Funds, select Deposit ZAR, choose Bank Transfer, enter the deposit amount, use the bank details and current reference code displayed by Binance, and transfer the exact amount from a bank account held in your name. Binance warns that the reference code is dynamic and can change between deposits — do not save an old reference and reuse it.

Absa Pay

Binance added official ZAR deposit and withdrawal instructions for Absa Pay in July 2026. If Absa Pay appears in your account, you can select it directly from the ZAR deposit screen and follow the payment instructions shown by Binance.

Capitec Pay

Binance introduced Capitec Pay as a more direct way for Capitec customers to fund their accounts in ZAR. Payment is approved through the Capitec app rather than by giving Binance your online-banking password. Binance has previously run temporary zero-fee promotions for Capitec Pay deposits, but promotional pricing should not be treated as permanent — check the fee displayed before confirming each deposit.

Card and Apple Pay

Binance also documents Card and Apple Pay for ZAR deposits. As of Binance’s August 2026 update, Google Pay is not supported for this particular ZAR deposit flow, even though it may still appear in parts of the interface. Check the final fee and amount to be received before paying.

Linked bank payments

Binance also maintains its linked-bank payment route for ZAR. The bank-account holder’s name must match the name on the Binance account. Supported banks and payment partners can change, so use Binance’s current deposit screen rather than relying on an old list of supported banks. For the bank-by-bank route, read How to deposit ZAR on Binance.

What about Binance welcome rewards?

Binance frequently runs new-user campaigns through its Rewards Hub and referral programmes. The headline value can change substantially between campaigns, countries and users. For that reason, it is better not to promise a fixed “100 USDT sign-up bonus” unless that exact offer appears on the registration or Rewards Hub page available to you.

Most welcome rewards are conditional. They can require actions such as completing identity verification, making a qualifying deposit, reaching a trading-volume target, completing the task within a deadline, or meeting country-specific eligibility rules. An “up to” amount is the maximum possible promotional value, not a guaranteed cash payment. Check the Rewards Hub after registration for the offer actually attached to your account.

Can you add GPYBA6R3 to an existing Binance account?

For an ordinary active Binance account, usually not. There is no general account setting that lets you freely replace an existing inviter or add a new referral code whenever you want.

However, Binance has an official Affiliate Recall Program for certain existing accounts. As of Binance’s March 2026 update, an eligible account generally needs to meet all of these conditions:

  • the Binance account already exists;
  • it has never been bound to an inviter;
  • the account is more than 90 days old; and
  • the account has not traded or used Binance products during the previous 90 days.

Binance changed the account-age and inactivity requirements from 180 days to 90 days on 24 March 2026. Recall is different from manually typing a referral code into account settings — it works through an eligible affiliate Recall link and Binance checks the account automatically.

If you already have an active Binance account, do not delete it and open another account simply to obtain a referral benefit. Check your eligibility under Binance’s current rules instead.

Is Binance regulated in South Africa?

This needs a more precise answer than simply saying “Binance is licensed” or “Binance is unlicensed.” South Africa regulates crypto-related financial services through the Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA), and Crypto Asset Service Providers are subject to the country’s licensing framework. At the same time, the legal entity involved can depend on the Binance product you are using.

For certain derivative products offered to South African users, Binance currently states that Nest Exchange Limited and Binance Bahrain BSC provide limited derivative products as juristic representatives of FiveWest OTC Desk (Pty) Ltd, an authorised South African financial services provider under FSP 51619. That wording matters — it does not mean that one South African authorisation automatically covers every Binance product or every Binance group entity. See Is Binance legal in South Africa? and FSCA & CASP licensing.

Before using a product where regulatory status matters to you, check the Binance entity named in that product’s Terms of Use, the product-specific disclosure shown on Binance, and the FSCA’s current register of authorised financial-services and crypto-asset providers.

Crypto tax and CARF in South Africa

A Binance referral rebate does not remove your South African tax obligations. SARS states that the tax treatment of crypto depends on the facts and circumstances. In broad terms, crypto profits may be treated either as revenue/income, where the activity is revenue in nature, or as capital gains, where the asset was held on capital account. There is no special rule saying that using a referral code makes gains tax-free.

CARF started in March 2026

South Africa implemented the Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF) from 1 March 2026. CARF is an international crypto-reporting standard developed by the OECD. Under the framework, qualifying Reporting Crypto-Asset Service Providers collect and report specified customer and crypto-transaction information to SARS.

The first South African CARF reporting period runs from 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027. The first provider returns for that period are due to SARS by 31 May 2027, with international exchanges of relevant CARF information expected from September 2027. CARF is a reporting framework, not a new crypto tax — individual taxpayers still need to declare taxable crypto activity through the normal tax system. See Crypto tax in South Africa.

Keep records of acquisition dates, ZAR acquisition cost, disposal dates, ZAR proceeds, trading fees, withdrawal and network fees where relevant, and transaction history.
