Tax & Regulation

Before you trade, know the rules

Two regulators matter most for South African crypto users.

SARS

Tax

Crypto profits may be treated differently depending on whether your activity is capital or revenue in nature. Keep records — you may need them.

FSCA

Regulation

Crypto asset service providers operate within South Africa's financial-services regulatory framework. Licensing tells you who is regulated locally.
